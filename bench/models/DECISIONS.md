| 6 | 2026-11-12 | New slot: scenes | The owner asked for scenes: all of a character's scenes, with the shots in which the character is not to be seen. It becomes an eighth slot. Fixed before any test: (1) the output is one boundary score per gap between two shots; scenes come from a threshold on it, so a coarser setting only merges scenes and never reshuffles them; a character's scenes are the scenes with at least one shot of that character, and the shots without them are marked. (2) The measure is the editor's: per main character, of the shots of the scenes that character is truly in, the share returned and the share of the returned ones that belong (the F of both), beside boundary F and scene overlap. (3) A ladder, each step only if the one before misses the gate: A, what a project already stores (who is in each shot, the frames' colour signature, shot lengths), with the rule that a shot between two alike shots belongs with them; this needs no new pass, so old projects get scenes without a re-analysis. B, a picture embedding per shot (three frames; a place network and a general one). C, who speaks across the cut. D, a small model (1 to 5 M weights) over 21 shots that reads the shots' likeness to one another, not their raw vectors, so that the encoder under it can change. (4) Nothing is trained while A to C pass. | Research 2026-11-12 (one web survey; notes outside the repository).
